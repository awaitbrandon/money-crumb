MoneyCrumb privacy policy

Effective 27 Oct 2026. Questions: contact the developer through the app or support information provided with the app.

The short version

MoneyCrumb stores your financial information on your device. There is no account required to use the app, and the app is not designed to sell your data or track your activity for advertising or analytics. Some features may require the app to connect to online services when needed.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter in the app — such as entries, amounts, names and notes, categories, accounts, balances, budgets, recurring items, favourites, saved selections and app settings — is kept in the app’s local storage on your device. This information is not sent to a MoneyCrumb account or server by default.

What goes online

  • Data needed for certain features. When the app needs current information such as exchange rates, price data, or service availability, it may contact online providers to fetch that data. These requests may include limited technical information such as the type of request, date or currency code, and the device’s internet address.
  • App updates and maintenance. The app may check whether a newer version is available and, if needed, download it. This can involve sending the current app version and basic technical details required to determine whether an update is available.
  • Other third-party services. If you use a feature that depends on an external service or AI tool, that service may receive the data needed to perform the requested task. You are responsible for reviewing and choosing the service you use.

Like any internet request, these services may see the device’s internet address and other standard technical information. Their own privacy policies govern how they use that information.

Backups and imports

  • When you export, share or send data from the app, it goes wherever you choose to send it. The app does not automatically upload your data elsewhere.
  • MoneyCrumb may keep local backups or snapshots on your device to help restore data. If your phone’s backup settings are enabled, the device operating system may also back up app data as part of normal device backup.
  • Files you import are processed on your device. If you use an AI or external conversion feature, any file you choose to send may be shared with the service you selected.

Permissions

  • Notifications: used only when you enable reminders or alerts for recurring items. These reminders are scheduled on your phone.
  • Internet and network access: used for online data and app updates when required by the app’s features.
  • Device features: such as vibration, startup scheduling, or keeping the device awake may be used to support reminders and app behaviour on your phone.

Deleting your data

Delete entries in the app, or uninstall MoneyCrumb to remove the data stored on the device. Backups or exported files you saved elsewhere remain under your control and are not managed by the app.

Children

MoneyCrumb is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
