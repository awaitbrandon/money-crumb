MoneyCrumb privacy policy

Effective 11 Nov 2026. MoneyCrumb is made by StackVerse. Questions: stackverse.app@gmail.com.

The short version

MoneyCrumb keeps what you log on your phone. There’s no account, no ads, no analytics, no automatic crash reporting and no MoneyCrumb server, so we don’t receive what you log. We receive feedback, or a crash report, only when you tap Send.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter: entries, amounts, names and notes, categories, accounts and balances, budgets, recurring bills, favourites, Pick for Me wheels and settings. It lives in a database inside the app. The name and photo on your profile card, if you add them, stay here too: the photo is copied into the app’s own storage from the picture you choose, and a backup file keeps your name but not the photo.

What goes online

  • Exchange rates from Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev). This happens when MoneyCrumb opens and its rates are more than a day old, when you tap Update rates in Currencies, and for past days whose entries still use an estimated rate. The requests contain currency codes and a date, never your amounts or anything you logged.
  • Exchange rates from the free currency-api (cdn.jsdelivr.net and currency-api.pages.dev), only when Frankfurter can’t be reached. These contain a currency code and a date.
  • App updates from Expo (u.expo.dev). When it opens, MoneyCrumb asks whether a newer version is available and downloads it if so. The request carries the app version and update channel, your phone’s operating system and a random install code, never anything you logged. Expo’s privacy policy (expo.dev/privacy) covers that service.
  • Feedback you send, through Discord (discord.com), only when you write feedback in Settings and tap Send, or tap Send report on the screen that shows after a crash. See “Feedback you send” below.
  • MoneyCrumb Plus, through RevenueCat (api.revenuecat.com) and Google Play. When MoneyCrumb opens, it checks whether you have Plus; buying and restoring go through Google Play’s own screens. RevenueCat gets a random app user code and your Plus purchases, never anything you logged, and MoneyCrumb never sees your card. RevenueCat’s privacy policy (revenuecat.com/privacy) covers that service.

Like any web request, these show the service your IP address. Their own privacy policies cover what they do with it.

Feedback you send

Send feedback in Settings posts your message to the MoneyCrumb team’s Discord, where only the team reads it. It carries what you wrote, the kind you picked (a problem, an idea or something else), the email or Discord name you add if you want a reply, and, unless you untick it, the app version and your phone’s model and system version. Nothing you logged is attached. Discord’s privacy policy (discord.com/privacy) covers how it stores the message. To have your feedback deleted, write to us.

If MoneyCrumb hits an error, the screen it shows offers Send report. It shows everything the report holds before you send it: the error and where in the app it happened, and the app version and your phone’s model and system version, plus a note if you write one. It goes to the same Discord, only when you tap Send report.

Joining the MoneyCrumb Discord is up to you. It opens Discord, and what you post there is visible to its members under Discord’s terms.

Backups and imports

  • When you export a backup or a CSV, or share a picture or text (a pick, your month, a habit or an item’s prices), it goes wherever you send it. We never receive it. A picture shows amounts only if you tick Show amounts.
  • MoneyCrumb keeps a snapshot of its data in its own storage, renewed daily and before an import or a merge. If your phone’s own backup is on (Google backup on Android, iCloud Backup on iPhone), it may copy the app’s data, snapshot included, to your Google or iCloud account, as it does for other apps. You can turn this off in your phone’s settings.
  • Files you import (a Money+ backup or a CSV) are read on your phone and not uploaded. If you use the AI prompt to convert another app’s export, you share that file with the AI service yourself, and it sees what’s in it.

Permissions

  • Pay through Google Play: so you can buy Plus.
  • Notifications: asked only when you turn on a reminder for a recurring bill. Reminders are scheduled on your phone.
  • On iPhone, that is the only permission MoneyCrumb asks for.
  • On Android, granted at install, without a prompt:
    • internet and network status, for exchange rates, app updates and feedback you send
    • vibration, for haptics
    • run at startup, so bill reminders are set again after a restart
    • keeping the phone awake, added by the notifications library that schedules bill reminders

Deleting your data

Delete entries in the app, or uninstall MoneyCrumb to remove everything it stored on the phone. Backups you exported stay where you saved them.

Children

MoneyCrumb is meant for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
