MoneyCrumb privacy policy

Effective 27 Oct 2026.

Overview

MoneyCrumb is designed to keep your financial information on your device whenever possible. We do not require you to create an account to use the app. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use the app to advertise to you.

Information we store

MoneyCrumb may store information you enter in the app, such as entries, amounts, names and notes, categories, accounts, balances, budgets, recurring items, favourites, saved selections, and app settings. This information is stored locally on your device and is generally not sent to a MoneyCrumb server.

Information we may send online

Some features may require the app to connect to online services to provide current information, perform updates, or access external tools. In those cases, the app may send limited data needed for the requested function, such as the request type, date, currency code, device technical information, and the device’s internet address. These third-party services may use that information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

App updates and external services

MoneyCrumb may check for app updates and download them when available. This may require sending the current app version and basic technical information to the service providing the update. If you use a feature that relies on a third-party service, that service may receive only the information required to complete the requested task.

Backups and sharing

When you export, share, or send data from the app, it is sent only where you choose to send it. The app does not automatically upload your information elsewhere. Files you import are processed on your device, and any data sent to a third-party service is only sent if you choose to use that feature.

Device permissions

MoneyCrumb may request permissions needed for app functionality, such as notifications, internet access, and device features needed to support reminders or other in-app behaviour. These permissions are used only to provide the services you enable.

Deleting your data

You can delete entries in the app or uninstall the app to remove the data stored on your device. Backups or exported files that you save elsewhere remain under your control and are not managed by the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
